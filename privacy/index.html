Candle Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE]

1. Who we are

1.1. This Privacy Policy explains how [Candle entity], [registered address], [company number] ("Candle", "we", "us") processes personal data when you use the Candle website at https://candle.trading and the Candle web application (the "Interface").

1.2. Candle is the controller of the personal data described in this policy, unless stated otherwise. We apply the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR").

1.3. Contact for privacy matters: legal@candle.trading. [Data protection officer / EU representative: [NAME or "not appointed" — counsel to confirm whether required].]

2. Our approach in short

  • We store as little as we can: an account identifier, your wallet address and the data needed to run the features you use.
  • We do not store your email address, name, wallet private key or payment card details.
  • We use no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • Trading on Hyperliquid is public by nature: your address, orders, trades and positions are visible to anyone on the blockchain, independently of Candle.

Usage events and how you found us. To see which parts of Candle are used and where our users come from, our own servers record first-party usage events: for example, that an analytics module was opened, that an order was sent through Candle (market, size and fee in dollars), or that a deposit address was shown and later credited. Events are linked to your Candle account ID or, before you sign in, to a random identifier kept in your browser's local storage; they contain no IP address, email, keys or other people's wallet addresses, and are never sent to third parties. Once, after you sign in, we also store how you first reached Candle (campaign tags such as utm_source, a referral code, the website you came from and the first page you opened) and, if you choose to answer, your reply to "How did you hear about Candle?". If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, it sends us no usage events. Usage events are kept for [400] days; how you found us is kept while your account exists.

3.2. Data stored only in your browser

In your browser's local storage we (or Privy's software) keep: your Privy session tokens, a referral code from a referral link (for 7 days), your interface settings, a random usage identifier and a record of how you first reached Candle (see "Usage events" above). If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, the last two are not created. This data stays on your device; see Section 10.

3.3. Data we do NOT collect or store

  • your email address or name — these are held by Privy, our authentication provider (see Section 6);
  • your wallet private key — it is managed by Privy's systems and never visible to Candle;
  • payment card or bank details — Candle does not take payments;
  • government ID or KYC documents;
  • advertising IDs, cross-site tracking data or third-party analytics data.

Support emails. If you write to support@candle.trading or another Candle address, we receive your email address and the content of your message in our mailbox. We use this only to answer you.

4. Sources of data

  • From you, when you sign in, configure features (DCA, tracking, alerts, feed), or contact us.
  • From Privy, which provides us with your Privy user ID and wallet address after you sign in.
  • From Hyperliquid's public blockchain and API, such as order statuses and the public positions of addresses (including addresses you track).
  • Automatically, from your browser's connection to our servers (technical logs).

6.2. Hyperliquid (public blockchain). When you trade, your signed orders are sent to Hyperliquid and recorded on its public blockchain. Hyperliquid is not our processor: data on its chain — your address, orders, trades, positions and balances — is public and can be seen, copied and analysed by anyone. Your browser also connects to Hyperliquid's public API directly to load market data, prices and your account, so Hyperliquid's infrastructure sees your IP address and the public addresses you view.

6.3. Deposit providers — only if you use them. If you choose to deposit through Relay, Unit or Circle (CCTP), those providers process your wallet addresses and transaction data under their own privacy policies. Candle does not send them additional personal data. When you open a deposit option, your browser requests supported assets, prices and deposit status from that provider directly, so it sees your IP address and wallet address.

6.4. Content and data providers — no user data. We obtain news, X posts and market data from Crypto News API, Stock News API, twitterapi.io and CoinGecko. We do not share your personal data with them.

6.5. Landing page hosting. Our landing page is temporarily hosted on GitHub Pages. Through it we only record one usage event per visit (see Section 3.1, "Usage events") and keep how you reached the page in your browser; GitHub may process technical connection data (such as IP addresses) as described in its own privacy statement.

6.6. Authorities and legal requirements. We may disclose data if required by law, court order or a competent authority, or where necessary to protect the rights, safety or property of Candle, our users or others.

6.7. Corporate transactions. If Candle is reorganised, merged or sold, data may be transferred to the successor, subject to this policy.

9. Your rights

9.1. Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and receive a copy;
  • rectification of inaccurate data;
  • erasure ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legal exceptions;
  • restriction of processing;
  • data portability — receive data you gave us in a machine-readable format;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent (for example, by disabling browser push notifications), without affecting earlier processing.

9.2. To exercise these rights, email legal@candle.trading from a channel we can verify, or follow the instructions in Account. We may ask you to prove control of your wallet address (for example, by signing a message). We will respond within one month, which may be extended where the law allows.

9.3. Limits for on-chain data. We can delete the data stored on our servers, but we cannot alter or delete your transactions, orders, positions or address history on Hyperliquid's public blockchain, which is outside our control. We may also need to keep certain records (for example, referral payouts or order logs) to meet legal obligations or to defend legal claims.

9.4. Complaints. You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live or work or where an alleged infringement occurred. 11. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS);
  • envelope encryption of trading keys, with key-encryption keys managed by AWS Key Management Service (KMS) in production;
  • never writing authorisation headers or keys to logs;
  • access to production systems and the admin panel restricted to the founder;
  • an audit log of administrative actions;
  • rate limiting and abuse protection.

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to you, we will notify the competent authority and, where required, you. Please report security issues to security@candle.trading.

12. Children

Candle is only for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used Candle, contact us and we will delete the related data.
