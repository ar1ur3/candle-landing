    Questions

    Before you ask.

    Is Candle an exchange?

    No. Trades execute on Hyperliquid, and your positions show up in any Hyperliquid interface.

    Which markets can I trade?

    Hyperliquid perpetuals, HIP-3 markets and spot.

    Where does the data come from?

    Prices, the order book and trades come straight from Hyperliquid. Liquidation levels and positioning are computed by Candle from open positions on Hyperliquid.

    Is there a mobile app?

    Not yet. Candle is built for desktop browsers. iOS and Android apps come later.

    How do I get into the beta?

    Leave your email and tell us where you trade now. Invites go out in small batches, by email.

    Can I invite other traders?

    Yes. You get 25% of the Candle fee paid by traders who join with your link.
