6. Trading key

6.1. What it is. To let you trade without confirming every order in a wallet pop-up, and to run scheduled buys, Candle's server generates a separate key (a Hyperliquid "agent" or "API wallet" key, the "Trading Key"). The Trading Key only works after you approve it on Hyperliquid from your own wallet.

6.2. What it can and cannot do. Once approved, the Trading Key can place, modify and cancel orders and change leverage and margin mode on your Hyperliquid account. The Trading Key cannot withdraw or transfer your funds.

6.3. What this means for you. Because the Trading Key can trade, an order placed with it — whether by you, by a scheduled plan, by a bug, or by someone who has improperly obtained the key — can open, increase or close positions and cause losses, even though it cannot withdraw funds.

6.4. How we store it. We store the Trading Key encrypted (envelope encryption, with keys managed by a cloud key-management service in production). Trading Keys are never written to logs. We use the Trading Key only to carry out instructions you give through the Interface or plans you have configured.

6.5. Expiry and revocation. A Trading Key may have an expiry date. You can revoke it at any time in Account, and you can also remove it directly on Hyperliquid. After revocation, Candle can no longer submit orders for you with that key, and your scheduled buys are paused. Revocation does not cancel orders that are already resting on Hyperliquid — cancel those separately if you want to.
